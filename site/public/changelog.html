What moved.

A newest-first record of meaningful Motion milestones that are already real. No open tasks or speculative promises.

Open work lives on the public roadmap; this page records only what has shipped.

  1. 29 Jul 2026

    Public presence

    Motion opened a window to the experiment.

    The public landing page shipped with a clear product story, honest prototype boundaries, privacy details, and agent-readable product information. The game, camera, and room routes remain private.

  2. 19 Jul 2026

    Evidence

    Prototype claims became easier to verify safely.

    Motion gained a privacy-safe evidence record that distinguishes source and simulator proof from physical-device and deployment proof—without collecting camera frames, movement samples, or device identifiers.

  3. 18 Jul 2026

    Foundation

    The first playable direction took shape.

    Motion established its browser game and iPhone host, then simplified the first proof into a single-device experience: movement is read on the phone, the game runs there, and the screen can be mirrored to a larger display.
