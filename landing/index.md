# Motion

Motion is an experiment in body-controlled games for people interested in new
ways to play.

## What it does

Motion uses an iPhone camera and Apple Vision to extract body position
on-device. Pose data becomes game input, the game renders on the phone, and the
phone may be mirrored to a television.

The player's body is the controller. No console or wearable sensor is required
by the current design.

## Privacy

Camera frames remain on the iPhone. They are not transmitted or stored. The
public landing page does not request camera access and contains no account
system or tracking pixel.

## Current proof

- Three game modes, a browser game, and an iPhone host are implemented.
- The iOS app compiles, launches, and renders in the simulator.
- Physical-iPhone camera tracking, joint mapping, mirroring, and ReplayKit
  recording remain unverified.
- Motion is not downloadable, publicly playable, or available in an app store.
