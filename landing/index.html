motion

One phone. No wearable.

The room becomes the controller.

Motion is built around a simple loop: see movement locally, turn it into pose input, and let the game answer immediately.

  1. iPhone

    Sees the movement

    The camera stays on the phone.

  2. Vision

    Finds the pose

    Only movement data drives the game.

  3. Game

    Answers on screen

    Play on the phone or mirror to a TV.

local

Private by construction

Your camera stays with you.

Motion uses Apple Vision on the iPhone to extract body position. Camera frames are not transmitted or stored. The public page does not ask for camera access, either.
